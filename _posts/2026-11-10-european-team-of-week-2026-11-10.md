<p data-draft-paragraph="1">Barcelona and Nottingham Forest produced the two biggest results of the European weekend, with a pair of outstanding individual performances leading the way.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="2">Barcelona went to Atlético Madrid and overcame an early deficit to win 2-1, keeping its three-point advantage over Real Madrid at the top of La Liga. Fermín López scored both goals, continuing his impressive development into one of the most dangerous attacking midfielders in Spain.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="3">In England, Nottingham Forest pulled off the weekend&#x27;s biggest upset, beating Manchester City 3-1 behind two goals from Liam Delap and an exceptional performance from young defender Jair Cunha. Forest entered the weekend in the relegation zone and without a home league goal all season. They managed three against one of Europe&#x27;s most talented teams.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="4">Elsewhere, Inter Milan delivered an impressive 2-0 victory over a strong Como side, Real Madrid comfortably handled Valencia and Bayern Munich survived a tricky road game against Mainz.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="5">This week&#x27;s Best XI lines up in an aggressive 4-2-4, with <strong>Fermín López earning Player of the Week honors</strong> ahead of Delap. As usual, the Player of the Week is a separate selection from the starting XI.</p>

<h2 id="player-of-the-week-fermin-lopez-barcelona" data-draft-paragraph="6"><strong>Player of the Week: Fermín López, Barcelona</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="7">There were two very strong candidates for this week&#x27;s award, but Fermín gets the nod after scoring both goals in Barcelona&#x27;s comeback victory at Atlético Madrid.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="8">This was an important game in the Spanish title race. Atlético entered on a seven-game winning streak and had an opportunity to overtake Barcelona at the top of the table with a victory.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="9">For much of the opening half, Atlético looked capable of doing exactly that.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="10">Diego Simeone&#x27;s team repeatedly exploited the space behind Barcelona&#x27;s aggressive defensive line, creating several dangerous opportunities before Giuliano Simeone opened the scoring in the tenth minute.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="11">Barcelona gradually worked its way back into the match, largely through Pedri&#x27;s ability to receive the ball and advance through midfield. Just before halftime, Pedri made an excellent dribbling run into the Atlético defense and found Fermín in the box. The pass took a fortunate deflection, but Fermín finished cleanly to equalize.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="12">The biggest tactical change came at halftime when Hansi Flick introduced Frenkie de Jong. Barcelona began moving the ball with greater composure and applying more effective pressure when they lost possession. Atlético continued to threaten occasionally, but they were no longer consistently finding the open spaces that had caused Barcelona so many problems early.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="13">Then Fermín delivered the winner in the 66th minute.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="14">Lamine Yamal found him making a sharp run into the penalty area with a perfectly weighted pass, and Fermín finished past Jan Oblak.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="15">There is a reasonable argument that Yamal&#x27;s pass was the best individual action in the game, and Pedri deserves considerable credit for helping Barcelona regain control. But Fermín&#x27;s movement was just as important.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="16">Barcelona&#x27;s attack is loaded with players who want the ball at their feet. Fermín provides something different with his willingness to make aggressive runs beyond the forwards and arrive in scoring positions from midfield.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="17">That ability has become increasingly valuable for a Barcelona team that no longer relies on a conventional center forward to occupy the opposition&#x27;s center backs.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="18">Delap&#x27;s two goals against Manchester City make this a close decision. However, Fermín scored both goals in a comeback victory away to a direct title rival, preserving Barcelona&#x27;s position at the top of La Liga.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="19"><strong>That&#x27;s enough to make him my Player of the Week.</strong></p>

<h2 id="goalkeeper-matz-sels-nottingham-forest" data-draft-paragraph="20"><strong>Goalkeeper: Matz Sels, Nottingham Forest</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="21">Nottingham Forest had gone the entire season without scoring a home league goal before Manchester City arrived Saturday. They finished the afternoon with three goals and three points, and Sels helped make the early advantage hold up.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="22">City had much more possession for substantial stretches, particularly after Rayan Cherki cut Forest&#x27;s lead to 2-1 early in the second half.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="23">Sels wasn&#x27;t required to produce a ridiculous number of spectacular saves. City managed only three shots on target despite creating several dangerous situations. Forest&#x27;s defenders deserve plenty of credit for that, particularly Jair Cunha.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="24">Still, Sels provided a dependable presence behind a defense that spent much of the second half dealing with an increasingly desperate City attack.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="25">Forest has struggled badly this season, and beating a team with City&#x27;s attacking talent required contributions throughout the lineup. Sels did his part in one of the most surprising results of the season.</p>

<h2 id="defender-djed-spence-inter-milan" data-draft-paragraph="26"><strong>Defender: Djed Spence, Inter Milan</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="27">Inter made quick work of Como&#x27;s impressive start to the Serie A season, scoring twice in the opening 19 minutes before settling into a comfortable 2-0 victory.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="28">Spence was directly responsible for the opener.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="29">Playing as the right wingback in Inter&#x27;s 3-5-2, he made an aggressive overlapping run after Marcus Thuram received the ball. Thuram recognized the movement and released Spence down the right, where he delivered a cross that Pio Esposito converted.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="30">It was an excellent example of how Inter&#x27;s wingbacks can create problems for opponents. With two central forwards occupying the center backs, Spence was able to attack the space outside Como&#x27;s defensive line and provide another dangerous runner.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="31">He continued to make forward runs, while also contributing to a defensive performance that limited Como to five shots and only one on target.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="32">Spence&#x27;s assist was the most obvious contribution, but the combination of attacking production and defensive stability gets him into this team.</p>

<h2 id="defender-mario-hermoso-roma" data-draft-paragraph="33"><strong>Defender: Mario Hermoso, Roma</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="34">Roma continued its excellent start to the Serie A season with a comfortable 3-0 victory over Sassuolo, and Hermoso was arguably the best player on the field.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="35">The Spanish defender opened the scoring and helped Roma maintain another clean sheet. Italian coverage was particularly enthusiastic, with Sky Sport naming him Man of the Match and awarding him an 8/10.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="36">Hermoso has always been more comfortable on the ball than many conventional center backs, and his ability to contribute to possession gives Roma another option when building attacks from deeper positions.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="37">Against Sassuolo, he combined that quality with a strong defensive performance and the opening goal that put Roma in control.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="38">Roma has quietly developed into one of Italy&#x27;s strongest teams this season. Its defensive organization has been a major reason, and Hermoso&#x27;s performance was another example of the contributions coming from the back line.</p>

<h2 id="defender-jair-cunha-nottingham-forest" data-draft-paragraph="39"><strong>Defender: Jair Cunha, Nottingham Forest</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="40">There might not have been a more surprising individual performance anywhere in Europe this weekend.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="41">Cunha opened the scoring against Manchester City after just six minutes, dispossessing Joško Gvardiol, driving forward and finishing past Gianluigi Donnarumma.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="42">A center back stealing the ball from a Manchester City defender and carrying it forward to score is not exactly the standard opening sequence for a team fighting relegation.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="43">But that play represented what Forest did so effectively throughout the first half.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="44">Rather than simply retreating into their own penalty area, Forest looked for opportunities to pressure City&#x27;s defenders when they were vulnerable. Cunha recognized one of those moments and turned a defensive intervention directly into a goal.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="45">His contribution didn&#x27;t end there.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="46">Late in the first half, Erling Haaland rounded Matz Sels and appeared certain to score, only for Cunha to recover and clear the ball off the line.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="47">That was every bit as important as his goal, preventing City from getting back into the game before halftime.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="48">Cunha was aggressive stepping forward, but he also demonstrated the recovery speed and defensive awareness necessary to deal with City&#x27;s attacking talent.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="49">A goal, a critical goal-line clearance and a victory over Manchester City make this one of the easiest defensive selections of the week.</p>

<h2 id="defender-manuel-akanji-inter-milan" data-draft-paragraph="50"><strong>Defender: Manuel Akanji, Inter Milan</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="51">Akanji joins Spence after helping Inter shut down one of Serie A&#x27;s more dangerous attacking teams.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="52">Como entered the weekend competing near the top of the table, but they struggled to create meaningful opportunities against Inter&#x27;s defense.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="53">The final possession statistics are a little misleading. Como actually finished with 57 percent of the ball, but generated only five shots and approximately 0.2 expected goals.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="54">Inter was perfectly comfortable allowing Como to circulate possession in areas that posed little danger.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="55">Akanji played on the right side of Inter&#x27;s back three, providing protection behind Spence when the wingback advanced. That balance allowed Inter to threaten down the right without becoming excessively vulnerable when possession changed hands.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="56">There were few spectacular individual defensive moments because Inter rarely allowed Como to create the situations that would require them.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="57">That is a pretty good outcome for a center back facing one of the better attacking teams in Italy.</p>

<h2 id="midfielder-joshua-kimmich-bayern-munich" data-draft-paragraph="58"><strong>Midfielder: Joshua Kimmich, Bayern Munich</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="59">Bayern Munich remained unbeaten in Bundesliga play with a 2-1 victory at Mainz, although it was not one of their more convincing attacking performances.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="60">Kimmich opened the scoring in the 14th minute after Harry Kane intercepted a poor pass and laid the ball into his path. Kimmich struck an excellent shot from outside the penalty area to give Bayern an early lead.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="61">The goal was the statistical highlight, but Kimmich&#x27;s larger contribution came from his work organizing Bayern&#x27;s possession.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="62">He and Aleksandar Pavlović anchored the midfield, allowing Bayern&#x27;s attacking players to push forward while maintaining control of the areas behind them.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="63">Mainz was generally able to make the game uncomfortable, limiting Bayern&#x27;s ability to turn extended possession into clear scoring chances. Kane eventually helped create a second goal for Lennart Karl, and Bayern survived a late Mainz goal to take all three points.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="64">Kimmich wasn&#x27;t the only candidate from Bayern. Kane assisted both goals and has become an increasingly important creator in addition to his ridiculous scoring production.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="65">But Kimmich gets the midfield spot for providing the opening goal while helping Bayern manage a difficult road game.</p>

<h2 id="midfielder-pedri-barcelona" data-draft-paragraph="66"><strong>Midfielder: Pedri, Barcelona</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="67">Pedri&#x27;s statistical line was considerably less impressive than several midfielders who missed this team.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="68">There is a good reason he is here anyway.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="69">Barcelona struggled badly with Atlético Madrid&#x27;s aggressive opening approach. Atlético was winning balls in midfield, attacking quickly into the space behind Barcelona&#x27;s defensive line and creating the better chances.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="70">Pedri was one of the few Barcelona players who consistently found ways to advance the ball through that pressure.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="71">His ability to receive in tight spaces, turn away from defenders and carry the ball forward helped Barcelona gradually establish a foothold in the match.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="72">The best example came just before halftime.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="73">Pedri drove through Atlético&#x27;s midfield and into the defensive line before finding Fermín in the penalty area for the equalizer. The pass benefited from a deflection, but the initial run was what broke Atlético&#x27;s structure.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="74">Barcelona improved substantially after Frenkie de Jong entered at halftime, giving Pedri another midfielder capable of controlling possession and helping the team escape pressure.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="75">That allowed Pedri to operate with greater freedom rather than carrying so much responsibility for advancing the ball himself.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="76">There were midfielders around Europe who scored more goals or recorded more assists. Few faced a more difficult opponent, and Pedri&#x27;s ability to change where the game was played was fundamental to Barcelona&#x27;s comeback.</p>

<h2 id="winger-arda-guler-real-madrid" data-draft-paragraph="77"><strong>Winger: Arda Güler, Real Madrid</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="78">Real Madrid continued its excellent run under José Mourinho with a comfortable 3-0 victory at Valencia, and Güler was the standout attacking performer.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="79">The Turkish midfielder scored Madrid&#x27;s second goal in the 36th minute, arriving in the box to finish Jude Bellingham&#x27;s cutback.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="80">He nearly added another before halftime, forcing an excellent save from Stole Dimitrievski with a powerful shot from outside the penalty area.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="81">In the second half, Güler provided the assist for Kylian Mbappé&#x27;s goal that completed the scoring.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="82">It was a particularly nice piece of individual play, as he controlled the ball, turned away from pressure and released Mbappé into a dangerous position.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="83">What has become increasingly interesting about Güler&#x27;s role under Mourinho is his ability to operate in the spaces around Madrid&#x27;s more explosive attackers.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="84">Mbappé and Diomande can stretch defenses with their speed, while Bellingham makes aggressive runs from midfield. Güler is comfortable finding pockets between those movements, receiving the ball and creating chances with quick combinations.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="85">Valencia offered relatively little resistance for much of the match, so this wasn&#x27;t as difficult an assignment as the ones faced by Fermín or Delap.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="86">Still, a goal, an assist and several additional scoring opportunities make Güler an easy selection.</p>

<h2 id="forward-marcus-thuram-inter-milan" data-draft-paragraph="87"><strong>Forward: Marcus Thuram, Inter Milan</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="88">Thuram was involved in both goals as Inter overwhelmed Como in the opening 20 minutes.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="89">His first contribution came in the 12th minute, when he recognized Djed Spence&#x27;s overlapping run and played the wingback into space to deliver the cross for Pio Esposito&#x27;s opener.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="90">Seven minutes later, Thuram scored himself.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="91">Como defender Álex Valle failed to clear the ball, and Thuram reacted quickly to finish with his right foot and double Inter&#x27;s advantage.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="92">The goal was relatively straightforward, but Thuram&#x27;s overall performance demonstrated the variety he brings to Inter&#x27;s attack.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="93">He can receive with his back to goal, run directly at defenders or drift toward the wing to create space for the other forward.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="94">His combination with Spence on the opening goal was particularly effective because Thuram&#x27;s movement drew defensive attention toward the middle before he released the ball into the space outside.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="95">Inter didn&#x27;t need much more after taking a 2-0 lead so early. Como actually had more possession for the remainder of the game, but rarely threatened to get back into it.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="96">Thuram helped establish the advantage that allowed Inter to control the game without needing to dominate the ball.</p>

<h2 id="forward-liam-delap-nottingham-forest" data-draft-paragraph="97"><strong>Forward: Liam Delap, Nottingham Forest</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="98">There is a very strong argument that Delap should be Player of the Week.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="99">The former Manchester City striker scored twice against his old club, helping Nottingham Forest produce a stunning 3-1 upset at the City Ground.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="100">His first goal arrived in the 18th minute, shortly after Jair Cunha had given Forest the lead. Delap made an excellent individual run before powering his finish past Donnarumma to double the advantage.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="101">City gradually recovered and began threatening Forest&#x27;s defense. When Rayan Cherki scored early in the second half, it appeared the visitors might be capable of salvaging something from the game.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="102">Then Delap struck again.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="103">Forest broke forward on a counterattack in the 73rd minute and Delap finished with his weaker foot to restore the two-goal lead and effectively seal the victory.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="104">The second goal was particularly important because City had taken control of possession and was creating more dangerous opportunities.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="105">Delap gave Forest an outlet when they regained the ball, using his physical presence and direct running to threaten City&#x27;s defensive line rather than allowing the visitors to simply recycle possession and attack again.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="106">Forest&#x27;s early-season struggles have been substantial, and Delap himself had scored only once in his first eight league appearances after arriving from Chelsea.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="107">Against Manchester City, he looked like the player Forest hoped they were signing.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="108">Two excellent goals, a major upset and three badly needed points.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="109">Fermín narrowly gets the top individual honor, but Delap is an extremely deserving runner-up.</p>

<h2 id="winger-lamine-yamal-barcelona" data-draft-paragraph="110"><strong>Winger: Lamine Yamal, Barcelona</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="111">Yamal didn&#x27;t score against Atlético Madrid, but he produced arguably the best pass of the weekend.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="112">With Barcelona having recovered from its difficult opening half, Yamal found Fermín making a run into the Atlético penalty area in the 66th minute.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="113">The pass was perfectly weighted, arriving in the space between Atlético&#x27;s defenders and goalkeeper Jan Oblak, allowing Fermín to finish without breaking his stride.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="114">It was an outstanding example of the vision that separates Yamal from almost every other winger in Europe.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="115">Most wide attackers are dangerous primarily because they can beat their defender and create space for a shot or cross.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="116">Yamal can do that, but he also recognizes opportunities through the middle that most wingers would never attempt.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="117">His ability to threaten defenders from the right wing forces opponents to devote extra attention to him. That creates opportunities for Barcelona&#x27;s midfielders to make runs into the spaces left behind.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="118">Fermín has become particularly effective at exploiting those openings.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="119">Yamal wasn&#x27;t flawless against Atlético. Barcelona&#x27;s attack struggled for substantial portions of the opening half, and there were moments when Atlético&#x27;s defensive pressure disrupted his rhythm.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="120">But once Barcelona gained more control of possession, he became increasingly dangerous.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="121">In a match against one of Spain&#x27;s strongest teams, Yamal delivered the moment of creativity that decided the result.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="124"><em><strong>Player of the Week: Fermín López, Barcelona</strong></em></p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="125">There are admittedly a couple of positional compromises in this lineup, particularly Hermoso at left back and Güler operating from the left wing.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="126">However, this was a weekend with too many outstanding attacking performances to force a more conservative formation.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="127">Barcelona earns the top individual honor after its important victory in Madrid, while Nottingham Forest and Inter each place three players among the twelve selections.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="128">Forest&#x27;s victory was the biggest surprise, Inter produced one of the most complete performances and Barcelona claimed the most consequential result in the Spanish title race.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="129">Fermín&#x27;s two goals made sure Barcelona came away with all three points. For this week, that gives him the edge over an excellent field.</p>

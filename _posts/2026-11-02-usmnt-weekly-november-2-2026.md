<p data-draft-paragraph="2">It was a particularly productive week for American midfielders, with Sebastian Berhalter delivering one of the best performances of his young European career and Christian Pulisic reminding everyone why AC Milan just committed to keeping him for another five years.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="3">Berhalter scored and assisted as Middlesbrough continued its impressive run atop the English Championship. Jack McGlynn matched that production for Stoke City, while Aidan Morris joined Berhalter in controlling the midfield during Middlesbrough&#x27;s comfortable victory.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="4">Pulisic provided one of the biggest moments of the weekend, opening the scoring in Milan&#x27;s 3-1 derby victory over Inter. He also signed a contract extension through 2031, a significant commitment to a player who has established himself as one of the club&#x27;s most dependable attackers.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="5">There was encouraging news among the American strikers as well. Folarin Balogun scored his first goal of the season for Monaco, while Ricardo Pepi returned from injury and needed only 13 minutes to find the net for PSV. Cole Campbell added another Bundesliga goal, and Sergiño Dest continued his productive season with an assist.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="6">Back in MLS, 17-year-old Adri Mehmeti quietly put together another exceptional week. The Red Bulls midfielder recorded three MLS-credited assists in two games, becoming the youngest player in club history to register multiple assists in one match. Unfortunately, New York lost both games, blowing a two-goal lead against Nashville on Sunday to officially eliminate itself from playoff contention.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="7">The biggest concerns remain injuries and playing time. Johnny Cardoso is sidelined again, Weston McKennie left Juventus&#x27; weekend match with adductor discomfort, and several younger Americans continue struggling to establish themselves in their European rotations.</p>

<h2 id="spotlight-players" data-draft-paragraph="17">Spotlight Players</h2>

<h3 id="sebastian-berhalter-middlesbrough" data-draft-paragraph="18"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/sebastian-berhalter.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Sebastian Berhalter, Middlesbrough</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="19">This may have been Berhalter&#x27;s best performance since moving to England.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="20">The 25-year-old midfielder scored and assisted in Middlesbrough&#x27;s 3-0 victory over Charlton on Saturday, helping the club maintain its position atop the Championship standings. More importantly, his contributions went well beyond the two scoring plays.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="21">Berhalter opened the scoring in the 12th minute after Amario Cozier-Duberry created space down the right wing and pulled the ball back toward the edge of the penalty area. Berhalter had positioned himself perfectly to receive the pass and struck a low shot into the corner.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="22">It wasn&#x27;t an unstoppable strike, and Charlton goalkeeper Arthur Okonkwo probably could have done better. However, Berhalter deserves credit for recognizing the space outside the box and getting himself into an excellent shooting position.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="23">His assist before halftime was even better.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="24">With Middlesbrough leading 1-0, Berhalter received the ball in midfield and slipped an excellent through ball between the Charlton defenders for Will Lankshear. The striker ran onto the pass and calmly lifted his finish over Okonkwo to double the advantage.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="25">The pass demonstrated one of Berhalter&#x27;s most valuable qualities. He recognized Lankshear&#x27;s movement early and played the ball into space rather than forcing the striker to slow down and receive it at his feet.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="26">Berhalter finished with six chances created, approximately 0.46 expected assists and 72 touches. Local reporter Joe Nicholson gave him a 9/10, one of the highest grades in Middlesbrough&#x27;s convincing victory.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="27">The six chances created are the most encouraging statistic. A long-range goal is nice, but creating that many opportunities suggests Berhalter was consistently finding ways to break down the opponent rather than simply benefiting from one spectacular moment.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="28">Aidan Morris deserves plenty of credit as well.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="29">The other American in Middlesbrough&#x27;s midfield completed the full 90 minutes and finished with 102 touches, helping his team maintain control of possession and recover the ball when attacks broke down. Nicholson awarded Morris an 8/10.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="30">The two Americans have complementary skill sets. Morris is particularly effective maintaining possession, protecting the middle of the field and providing an outlet when teammates face pressure. Berhalter has more freedom to advance into dangerous spaces, find attacking runners and contribute from set pieces.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="31">That combination is working extremely well for Middlesbrough.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="32">From a USMNT perspective, Berhalter&#x27;s development is especially interesting because the national team has plenty of athletic midfielders but fewer players who consistently generate chances from central positions.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="33">His ability to deliver dangerous set pieces was already established during his time in MLS. What he needs to demonstrate in England is that he can also influence games through his passing, movement and decision-making in open play.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="34">Saturday was excellent evidence that he can.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="35">Middlesbrough is pushing for promotion to the Premier League, and Berhalter is increasingly looking like an important part of that campaign. If he continues performing at this level, Mauricio Pochettino will have a difficult time keeping him out of the national-team midfield conversation.</p>

<h3 id="christian-pulisic-ac-milan" data-draft-paragraph="36"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/christian-pulisic.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Christian Pulisic, AC Milan</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="37">Apparently signing a five-year contract extension is a pretty good way to prepare for one of your club&#x27;s biggest games of the season.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="38">Pulisic agreed to a new contract with AC Milan through June 2031 on Friday, then celebrated by scoring the opening goal in Saturday&#x27;s 3-1 victory over Inter.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="39">The goal came after only four minutes and was an excellent example of how Pulisic has evolved as an attacking player.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="40">Diego Moreira found space on the right wing and delivered a cross toward the far side of the six-yard box. Pulisic recognized the opportunity, moved into the area behind Inter&#x27;s defenders and directed a header into the bottom corner.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="41">Milan doubled the lead just six minutes later through Adrien Rabiot. Inter pulled one back in the 22nd minute, but Moreira restored the two-goal advantage with an outstanding free kick shortly before halftime.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="42">Milan eventually held on comfortably enough to record its fourth consecutive derby victory over Inter.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="43">Pulisic played approximately 82 minutes and received an 8/10 from Milan-focused journalist Isak Möller, who praised his positioning and ability to connect midfield possession to attacking opportunities. The American wasn&#x27;t flawless on the ball, but his movement consistently gave Milan another option between Inter&#x27;s midfield and defensive lines. He was consistently dropping back to link up Milan’s midfield to its attackers. </p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="44">The difference from Milan&#x27;s midweek 4-2 defeat against Bologna was significant.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="45">Pulisic came off the bench in that game with Milan already struggling to establish meaningful attacking possession. He dropped deeper to receive the ball and attempted to generate something against a defense that had become increasingly comfortable protecting its lead. He also forced a save with a late header.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="46">The performance wasn&#x27;t poor, but there was only so much he could accomplish in a disjointed team performance.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="47">For the USMNT, the best development is that Pulisic appears to be returning to his usual form after the injuries and fitness issues that disrupted his summer.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="48">He has now scored three goals this season despite barely featuring during the opening month.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="49">The American player pool is changing rapidly, but Pulisic remains its most accomplished attacking player.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="50">Based on his recent performances, that isn&#x27;t changing anytime soon.</p>

<h3 id="folarin-balogun-monaco" data-draft-paragraph="51"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/folarin-balogun.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Folarin Balogun, Monaco</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="52">Balogun finally has his first goal of the season, and the performance that produced it may have been more encouraging than the goal itself.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="53">He came on at halftime Sunday with Monaco trailing Paris FC 2-0. Monaco had been thoroughly outplayed during the opening 45 minutes, struggling to create dangerous chances while Paris repeatedly found space behind its defense.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="54">Manager Filipe Luís responded by changing the formation to a 3-4-3 and introducing Balogun in place of Paris Brunner. It was an aggressive adjustment designed to give Monaco more attacking options and a better chance of getting the ball into dangerous positions.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="59">Monaco eventually lost 3-1 after Paris FC scored its third goal in the 74th minute.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="60">The result was disappointing, and Balogun probably should have done better with at least one of his later chances. Still, the overall attacking performance was positive. What stood out was how frequently he found dangerous positions.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="61">Balogun wasn&#x27;t simply standing between the center backs waiting for a perfect cross. He moved across the attacking line, attacked spaces near the six-yard box and made himself available as Monaco attempted to play more directly after halftime.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="62">That is the sort of performance he needs to reproduce.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="63">For much of his career, Balogun&#x27;s biggest strength has been his ability to attack space behind defenders. He is fast, capable of finishing with either foot and comfortable receiving passes while running toward goal.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="64">The challenge has been developing a more complete center-forward game, particularly against defenses that don&#x27;t provide much room to run.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="65">He needs to become more consistent with his movement inside the box, his timing on crosses and his ability to turn limited opportunities into goals.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="66">Sunday offered some encouragement in those areas.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="67">Of course, the larger situation at Monaco remains complicated. Balogun has struggled to establish himself as the unquestioned starting striker this season, and his transfer complications have allowed others to accumulate minutes in his absence.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="68">One goal off the bench does not suddenly settle that competition, but it gives Filipe Luís a reason to increase his involvement, particularly after Monaco&#x27;s initial attacking combination struggled so badly against Paris FC.</p>

<h3 id="adri-mehmeti-new-york-red-bulls" data-draft-paragraph="69"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/adri-mehmeti.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Adri Mehmeti, New York Red Bulls</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="70">The Red Bulls had a miserable week, but their 17-year-old midfielder is becoming increasingly difficult to ignore.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="71">Mehmeti recorded three MLS-credited assists across two games as New York suffered consecutive 3-2 defeats against New England and Nashville.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="72">Against New England on Wednesday, the Red Bulls fell behind 2-0 before launching an impressive second-half comeback. Mehmeti helped create the first goal in the 51st minute, participating in the possession that eventually allowed Cade Cowell to cut the deficit in half.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="75">The two “MLS assists” also established a club record. At 17 years and 205 days old, Mehmeti became the youngest player in Red Bulls history to record multiple “assists” in a single MLS match.</p>

<h3 id="gio-reyna-strasbourg" data-draft-paragraph="238">Gio Reyna, Strasbourg</h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="239">Reyna started again Sunday as Strasbourg defeated Auxerre 5-1.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="240">The American played approximately 66 minutes, completing 23 of 26 passes without registering a goal or assist.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="241">It was a relatively quiet individual performance in a convincing team victory.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="242">Reyna continues to receive opportunities in different attacking positions, although his best work still appears to come when he can operate centrally and receive between the lines.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="243">The positive remains his availability.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="244">After years of injuries and inconsistent club minutes, Reyna is regularly getting onto the field and building rhythm.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="245">However, the next stage of his development needs to involve more consistent attacking influence, not simply remaining healthy enough to play.</p>

<h3 id="mark-mckenzie-toulouse" data-draft-paragraph="246"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/mark-mckenzie.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Mark McKenzie, Toulouse</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="247">McKenzie started and played approximately 90 minutes in Toulouse&#x27;s 2-0 defeat against Marseille.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="248">It was a difficult defensive assignment against a team with several dangerous attacking options.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="249">Toulouse struggled to establish sustained attacking possession, leaving its defenders with substantial responsibility protecting space and attempting to advance the ball under pressure.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="250">McKenzie has demonstrated enough quality this season to remain an interesting national-team option, but this result didn&#x27;t provide much evidence of further progress.</p>

<h3 id="bryan-reynolds-rennes" data-draft-paragraph="251"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/bryan-reynolds.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Bryan Reynolds, Rennes</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="252">Reynolds came off the bench at halftime Friday as Rennes lost 5-2 to Nice.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="253">The American entered as part of a substantial tactical reshuffle after Rennes struggled badly during the opening 45 minutes.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="254">Unfortunately, the changes did little to solve the team&#x27;s defensive problems.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="255">Reynolds continues to offer attacking ability from the right side, particularly when allowed to play as a wingback.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="256">However, his defensive decision-making remains inconsistent, and Rennes&#x27; recent performances have provided several examples of why that matters.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="257">He is getting opportunities, but he needs to turn them into more reliable performances.</p>

<h2 id="eredivisie" data-draft-paragraph="258">Eredivisie</h2>

<h3 id="sergino-dest-psv" data-draft-paragraph="259"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/sergino-dest.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Sergiño Dest, PSV</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="260">Dest was involved in one of the more interesting tactical developments of the week as PSV demolished Willem II 7-0.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="261">Manager Peter Bosz deliberately pushed Dest farther forward, using Lutsharel Geertruida behind him to provide additional defensive stability.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="262">This wasn&#x27;t simply the normal situation where Dest occasionally overlapped from right back. PSV intentionally used him higher up the field to create width and stretch the defense.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="263">The move worked extremely well.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="264">Dest provided an assist for Sven Mijnans in the 50th minute and was involved in several additional attacking opportunities.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="265">Bosz subsequently explained that the adjustment was designed to make PSV&#x27;s attacking structure more dangerous.</p>

<h3 id="ricardo-pepi-psv" data-draft-paragraph="266"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/ricardo-pepi.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Ricardo Pepi, PSV</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="267">Pepi finally returned from injury Saturday and immediately reminded PSV what it had been missing.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="268">The American entered in the 60th minute against Willem II and scored just 13 minutes later, heading home PSV&#x27;s fifth goal in an eventual 7-0 victory.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="269">His manager seemed particularly happy to have him back.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="270">Bosz explained after the match that PSV had been creating plenty of chances without a natural striker but sometimes struggled to convert them.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="271">Pepi provides the penalty-area movement and finishing instincts that make those opportunities more dangerous.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="272">That is an encouraging endorsement, especially after the injury that interrupted his season in September.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="273">The next step is gradually increasing his minutes and demonstrating he can remain productive once he returns to the starting lineup.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="274">With Balogun also scoring this weekend, the competition for the USMNT striker position is starting to become interesting again.</p>

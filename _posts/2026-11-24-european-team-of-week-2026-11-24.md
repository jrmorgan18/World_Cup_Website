<p data-draft-paragraph="2">It was a particularly good weekend for attacking players in Serie A. Francesco Pio Esposito scored a hat trick in a wild 4-4 draw between Inter and Atalanta, Charles De Ketelaere scored twice to help Atalanta come back, and Donyell Malen scored four goals in Roma&#x27;s 5-1 win at Parma.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="3">In England, a pair of teams that have returned to the Premier League over the past two seasons delivered some of the biggest upsets of the weekend. Leeds went to Stamford Bridge and beat Chelsea 4-1, while Hull City knocked off second-place Brighton with the help of an outstanding goalkeeping performance. Liverpool also took advantage of an early Manchester United red card, with Florian Wirtz producing one of his best creative performances of the season.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="4">In Spain, Barcelona came from behind against Villarreal, with Pedri once again instrumental in turning the tide of a game.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="5">This week&#x27;s Best XI lines up in a 4-3-3, with Inter&#x27;s Francesco Pio Esposito narrowly edging Malen for Player of the Week honors.</p>

<figure class="soccer-article-figure soccer-article-figure--portrait soccer-article-figure--uncropped"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/euro-team-of-week-2026-11-24.png' | relative_url }}" alt="European Weekend Team of the Week Best XI, with Francesco Pio Esposito of Inter Milan as Player of the Week" width="1024" height="1536" loading="lazy"></figure>

<h2 id="player-of-the-week-francesco-pio-esposito-inter-milan" data-draft-paragraph="6"><strong>Player of the Week: Francesco Pio Esposito, Inter Milan</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="7">It&#x27;s difficult to give Player of the Week to someone other than a player who scored four goals, but the quality of opposition and circumstances of Esposito&#x27;s hat trick give him the slight edge.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="8">The young Italian striker scored three times in the opening 52 minutes against Atalanta in Bergamo. He opened the scoring in the 16th minute, doubled Inter&#x27;s advantage in the 32nd and restored their two-goal cushion shortly after halftime.</p>

<figure class="soccer-article-figure soccer-article-figure--uncropped"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/euro-team-of-week-2026-11-24/pio-esposito-atalanta-header-goal.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Pio Esposito heads in Inter’s second goal against Atalanta" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Esposito scores his second goal in Bergamo. Photo: <a href="https://www.channelnewsasia.com/sport/esposito-hat-trick-not-enough-atalanta-hold-inter-in-eight-goal-thriller-6573451" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Matteo Ciambelli / Reuters</a>.</figcaption></figure>

<p data-draft-paragraph="9">Esposito consistently found openings around Atalanta&#x27;s defenders, demonstrating an impressive understanding of how to position himself in the box. For a big striker, he is particularly adept at finding pockets of space rather than relying exclusively on his physical advantages over defenders.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="10">Unfortunately for Esposito, Inter couldn&#x27;t defend the advantage he repeatedly provided them. Atalanta clawed their way back into the game behind a pair of goals from Charles De Ketelaere and eventually equalized on a stoppage-time penalty from Franck Kessié.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="11">There is an argument against giving the award to a player whose team failed to win, particularly when Malen scored four times in a convincing Roma victory. However, it&#x27;s difficult to envision what more Esposito could have done to put Inter in position to win this game.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="12">The larger storyline is the continued emergence of Esposito as one of the most exciting young strikers in Europe. Producing a hat trick away to Atalanta is a pretty good indication of how far along he already is in his development.</p>

<h2 id="goalkeeper-konstantinos-tzolakis-hull-city" data-draft-paragraph="13"><strong>Goalkeeper: Konstantinos Tzolakis, Hull City</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="14">Hull City&#x27;s 2-0 victory over Brighton was one of the more misleading scorelines of the Premier League weekend.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="15">Brighton entered the game second in the Premier League and created plenty of opportunities to take the lead, but Tzolakis repeatedly denied them. The Greek goalkeeper finished with seven saves, keeping Hull level long enough for the game to turn dramatically in the final moments.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="16">After Brighton was reduced to ten men late in the game, Oli McBurnie scored twice in stoppage time to give Hull an improbable three points.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="17">McBurnie will get plenty of attention for his late heroics, but Hull would almost certainly have been chasing the game by that point without Tzolakis.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="18">The Greek keeper has already made a few appearances in our Team of the Week this season. His performances are a big reason Hull has been able to pull off some impressive results against the Premier League&#x27;s more talented teams.</p>

<h2 id="right-back-achraf-hakimi-paris-saint-germain" data-draft-paragraph="19"><strong>Right Back: Achraf Hakimi, Paris Saint-Germain</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="20">PSG had a difficult time putting away Nice on the road, eventually emerging with a 3-2 victory thanks in large part to the attacking contributions of Hakimi.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="21">The Moroccan right back assisted Désiré Doué&#x27;s go-ahead goal and was also involved in the sequence that earned PSG a penalty.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="22">Hakimi remains one of the most difficult fullbacks in Europe to account for defensively. His speed allows him to make runs beyond the forwards, and his timing and technical ability make those runs more than just an additional body in the attack.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="23">Nice managed to make PSG uncomfortable for much of the game, but Hakimi&#x27;s ability to provide another route into the attacking third helped the defending champions generate enough offense to escape with three points.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="24">Como&#x27;s Yan Couto had a strong argument for this spot after recording two assists and contributing to a clean sheet, but Hakimi gets the nod for his contributions in the more difficult matchup.</p>

<h2 id="center-back-jacobo-ramon-como" data-draft-paragraph="25"><strong>Center Back: Jacobo Ramón, Como</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="26">Como continued its impressive season with a 2-0 victory over Cagliari, with Ramón contributing at both ends of the field.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="27">The young Spanish center back scored the opening goal and was excellent defensively as Como maintained its advantage. Eurosport awarded him an 8.5 for his performance.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="28">Ramón is an interesting player in the context of Cesc Fàbregas&#x27; system. Playing center back for Como requires more than being a good traditional defender. The position demands composure in possession and the ability to defend away from the penalty area when the team commits numbers forward.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="29">Ramón&#x27;s development since leaving Real Madrid has been encouraging, and performances like this demonstrate why he is such an intriguing young defensive prospect.</p>

<h2 id="center-back-malick-thiaw-newcastle-united" data-draft-paragraph="30"><strong>Center Back: Malick Thiaw, Newcastle United</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="31">Newcastle earned a hard-fought point against Premier League leaders Arsenal in a 2-2 draw, with Thiaw scoring one of the Newcastle goals and delivering a strong defensive performance.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="32">Arsenal presents one of the more difficult assignments for opposing center backs. They have the technical quality to combine through the middle, dangerous players on both wings and midfielders who are consistently looking to make late runs into the box.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="33">Thiaw acquitted himself well against that collection of attacking talent while also making a significant contribution at the other end.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="34">There were several center backs who maintained clean sheets this weekend, but the quality of the opponent carries some extra weight here. Contributing a goal and helping Newcastle take points off the league leaders gets Thiaw into the team.</p>

<h2 id="left-back-gerard-martin-barcelona" data-draft-paragraph="35"><strong>Left Back: Gerard Martín, Barcelona</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="36">Martín was one of Barcelona&#x27;s best players in their comeback victory over Villarreal, scoring the goal that started the turnaround and making several important defensive interventions.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="37">Barcelona put itself in a difficult position early when a poor turnover from Fermín López helped Villarreal take the lead. Martín got Barcelona back into the game, getting on the end of a well-delivered cross from Pedri.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="38">While the goal was obviously important, his defensive work was equally encouraging.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="39">Martín doesn&#x27;t have the same physical tools as some of Barcelona&#x27;s other defenders, but he has increasingly demonstrated good awareness and positioning. Those are particularly valuable traits in Hansi Flick&#x27;s aggressive system, where defenders are frequently asked to make decisions with considerable space behind them.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="40">Martín has developed into a useful option at both left back and center back for Barcelona, and his versatility allows us to slot him into the left back position in this week&#x27;s team.</p>

<h2 id="midfielder-pedri-barcelona" data-draft-paragraph="41"><strong>Midfielder: Pedri, Barcelona</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="42">It&#x27;s becoming increasingly difficult to leave Pedri out of this team in any week where Barcelona plays a meaningful opponent.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="43">Villarreal took advantage of some sloppy play from Barcelona early, but Pedri became increasingly influential as Barcelona worked its way back into the match.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="44">His most tangible contribution was the excellent cross that set up Gerard Martín&#x27;s goal. However, his larger contribution was helping Barcelona regain control of the middle of the field and spend more of the game attacking a retreating Villarreal defense.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="45">What separates Pedri from most other possession-oriented midfielders is how often he advances the ball rather than simply maintaining possession. He can draw a defender toward him before finding a pass into the vacated space or carry the ball through the opening himself.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="46">Barcelona has plenty of players capable of producing spectacular moments in the attacking third, but Pedri is often the player responsible for getting them into those positions with the defense already compromised.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="47">This was another good example.</p>

<h2 id="midfielder-scott-mctominay-napoli" data-draft-paragraph="48"><strong>Midfielder: Scott McTominay, Napoli</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="49">Napoli needed only one goal to get past Torino, and not surprisingly, it came from Scott McTominay.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="50">The Scottish midfielder has developed into one of the most effective goalscoring midfielders in Europe since leaving Manchester United. His ability to arrive in the penalty area from deeper positions gives Napoli an attacking dimension that is difficult for opposing defenses to account for.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="51">McTominay isn&#x27;t going to control the tempo of a game like Pedri or create chances with the frequency of Florian Wirtz, but his movement and physical presence provide a different set of problems for defenders.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="52">Against a team like Torino, where opportunities can be difficult to manufacture, having a midfielder capable of providing another legitimate scoring threat is especially valuable.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="53">McTominay supplied the decisive moment in a tight game and was widely regarded as one of Napoli&#x27;s best performers by the Italian media.</p>

<h2 id="midfielder-florian-wirtz-liverpool" data-draft-paragraph="54"><strong>Midfielder: Florian Wirtz, Liverpool</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="55">From a statistical perspective, Wirtz may have produced the best midfield performance of the weekend.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="56">The German recorded a goal, an assist and an absurd ten chances created against Manchester United.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="57">There is a significant caveat to those numbers. United was reduced to ten men in just the eighth minute, giving Liverpool a numerical advantage for nearly the entire game.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="58">That certainly afforded Wirtz more space and opportunities than he would ordinarily find against United, but creating ten chances in a Premier League game is impressive under just about any circumstances.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="59">Wirtz is particularly effective when he can drift around the attacking third, find pockets between the opposition&#x27;s midfield and defensive lines and quickly combine with the players around him. Playing against ten men gave him more opportunities to do exactly that.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="60">The red card keeps him out of the Player of the Week discussion, but the sheer volume of quality attacking production comfortably earns him a spot in the XI.</p>

<h2 id="forward-noah-okafor-leeds-united" data-draft-paragraph="61"><strong>Forward: Noah Okafor, Leeds United</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="62">Leeds delivered perhaps their most impressive result since returning to the Premier League, going to Stamford Bridge and beating Chelsea 4-1.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="63">Okafor was the most dangerous player in the Leeds attack, scoring twice as they turned an early deficit into a comfortable victory.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="64">Chelsea&#x27;s lead lasted only three minutes before Okafor responded with an excellent finish. After Leeds took the lead, Okafor scored again to put them two goals clear.</p>

<figure class="soccer-article-figure soccer-article-figure--uncropped"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/euro-team-of-week-2026-11-24/noah-okafor-chelsea.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Noah Okafor applauds after Leeds United’s victory at Chelsea" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Noah Okafor after Leeds’ win at Stamford Bridge. Photo: <a href="https://www.leedsunited.com/en/news/okafor-takes-the-honours-after-unforgettable-stamford-bridge-showing" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Leeds United</a>.</figcaption></figure>

<p data-draft-paragraph="65">The timing of those goals was particularly impressive. His first prevented Chelsea from establishing control of the game after going ahead, while his second substantially changed the complexion of the match by giving Leeds some breathing room.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="66">Okafor&#x27;s combination of speed, directness and finishing ability makes him particularly dangerous against teams that commit numbers forward and leave space for opponents to attack in transition.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="67">Chelsea&#x27;s recent struggles take a little shine off the result, but a team winning 4-1 at Stamford Bridge is still remarkable.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="68">Okafor would have been a worthy Player of the Week in most weeks that didn&#x27;t feature multiple outstanding scoring performances in Italy.</p>

<h2 id="forward-charles-de-ketelaere-atalanta" data-draft-paragraph="69"><strong>Forward: Charles De Ketelaere, Atalanta</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="70">While Esposito was having the best goalscoring game of his young Serie A career, De Ketelaere was doing his best to ruin it.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="71">The Belgian scored twice in Atalanta&#x27;s 4-4 draw with Inter, with both goals coming when Atalanta trailed by two.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="72">De Ketelaere&#x27;s development at Atalanta has been one of the more interesting career turnarounds in Serie A. After struggling to find an effective role at AC Milan, he has become an excellent attacking player capable of influencing games from several different positions.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="73">He is an unusual combination of size and technical ability. He can occupy defenders centrally, drift into midfield to help connect the attack or find space around another striker. His movement makes him particularly difficult to track when the players around him are constantly interchanging positions.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="74">Inter looked to be in a commanding position on multiple occasions, but De Ketelaere kept providing Atalanta with a way back into the game.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="75">His two goals were instrumental in salvaging a point against one of the best teams in Italy.</p>

<h2 id="striker-donyell-malen-roma" data-draft-paragraph="76"><strong>Striker: Donyell Malen, Roma</strong></h2>

<p data-draft-paragraph="77">Four goals. Five shots. No penalties.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="78">It&#x27;s difficult to produce a much more efficient performance than Malen did in Roma&#x27;s 5-1 victory at Parma.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="79">While the final score suggests Roma had an easy afternoon, Malen&#x27;s first two goals came when the game was very much in the balance. He opened the scoring and then restored Roma&#x27;s advantage after Parma equalized.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="80">He subsequently added two more as Roma pulled away.</p>

<figure class="soccer-article-figure soccer-article-figure--uncropped"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/euro-team-of-week-2026-11-24/donyell-malen-parma.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Donyell Malen runs in celebration during Roma’s win at Parma" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Donyell Malen celebrates at the Stadio Tardini. Photo: <a href="https://www.asroma.com/en/news/76250/malen-bags-four-as-roma-crush-parma-5-1" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AS Roma</a>.</figcaption></figure>

<p data-draft-paragraph="81">Malen has become an excellent fit for Gian Piero Gasperini&#x27;s attack. His speed and willingness to make runs behind the defense complement Roma&#x27;s collection of creative attacking players, while his ability to operate from wider positions makes him more difficult to track than a conventional center forward.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="82">What has been particularly impressive about Malen at Roma is his evolution into such an efficient finisher. He has always possessed the athleticism to get into dangerous positions, but his production in front of goal has become increasingly reliable.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="83">The relative quality of the opposition is the only reason Malen isn&#x27;t the Player of the Week. From a pure goalscoring perspective, nobody in Europe had a better weekend.</p>

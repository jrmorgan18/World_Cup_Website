<p data-draft-paragraph="2">The first international window of the new World Cup cycle provided plenty of reasons for optimism about the next generation of American players. This weekend, several of those youngsters returned to their clubs and picked up right where they left off.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="3">Cavan Sullivan set up two more goals for Philadelphia, setting a new league record for goal contributions by a teenager. Julian Hall scored again for the New York Red Bulls, while fellow USMNT newcomer Adri Mehmeti helped create another goal in a convincing victory over San Diego.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="4">There were encouraging performances from the Americans in Spain as well. Johnny Cardoso continued his emergence as a regular starter at Atlético Madrid, delivering one of his best performances since returning from injury. Alex Freeman played another full 90 minutes for Villarreal and earned positive reviews despite a narrow defeat at Real Madrid.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="5">The best statistical performance of the weekend, however, belonged to Bryan Reynolds. Making his first Ligue 1 start for Rennes, the right back provided two assists in a 4-1 win over Auxerre. Joe Scally also had a strong individual performance, scoring for Borussia Mönchengladbach in a derby loss to Köln.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="6">The news wasn&#x27;t quite as positive everywhere. Weston McKennie was removed at halftime for Juventus, Gio Reyna dropped out of Strasbourg&#x27;s starting lineup and Cole Campbell found himself on the bench after starting Elversberg&#x27;s previous Bundesliga games.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="7">Meanwhile, the injuries to Ricardo Pepi, Gianluca Busio, Haji Wright and Kevin Paredes continue to interrupt their seasons.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="13"><strong>Up:</strong> Cardoso and Reynolds delivered the two strongest European performances. Cardoso increasingly looks like a natural part of Atlético&#x27;s midfield rather than a player getting occasional opportunities, while Reynolds made a compelling case for more playing time at Rennes. Freeman&#x27;s steady improvement in Spain continued, and Scally added a goal to an adventurous performance against Köln.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="14">In MLS, Sullivan&#x27;s production is becoming difficult to comprehend for a 17-year-old. Hall is also making a very convincing argument that his recent USMNT performances were no fluke, while Mehmeti continues to develop into one of the more interesting young midfielders in the American pool.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="15"><strong>Encouraging:</strong> Pulisic was dangerous despite going without a goal or assist, Richards played well for the most part in Palace&#x27;s victory and Dest produced another solid 90 minutes for PSV. McKenzie had a mixed defensive performance but won an important penalty as Toulouse fought back against Monaco.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="16"><strong>Mixed:</strong> Musah struggled in a difficult tactical assignment for Milan before improving after halftime. Adams played the entire match in Bournemouth&#x27;s 5-1 defeat at Chelsea, but the collective collapse makes individual judgments difficult. Tillman and Balogun remain involved at their clubs without consistently starting, while Tessmann and Aaronson continue searching for larger roles. Reyna and Campbell both being dropped from the starting lineup may be due to their travel for the international games, so we’ll monitor, but not get overly concerned.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="17"><strong>Concern:</strong> McKennie&#x27;s halftime substitution reflects a legitimate problem with his current attacking role at Juventus. Weah served a suspension, while several other Americans are still dealing with injuries or frustratingly limited playing time.</p>

<h2 id="spotlight-players" data-draft-paragraph="19">Spotlight Players</h2>

<h3 id="alex-freeman-villarreal" data-draft-paragraph="20"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/alex-freeman.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Alex Freeman, Villarreal</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="21">Freeman&#x27;s rapid transition from occasional substitute to regular La Liga starter continued Saturday with perhaps his most difficult assignment yet.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="22">The 22-year-old played the full 90 minutes at the Santiago Bernabéu as Villarreal fell 1-0 to Real Madrid. Although the result was disappointing, Freeman produced another generally positive performance against one of the best teams in Europe.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="23">Playing at right back, Freeman was matched up frequently against Yan Diomandé, one of Madrid&#x27;s dynamic young attackers. Villarreal-focused reporter Javier Mata of <em>AS</em> praised his performance in that matchup and highlighted his willingness to advance into attacking positions.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="26">Freeman has exceptional physical tools for a fullback. His size makes him difficult to overpower defensively, while his speed allows him to get forward and recover quickly when possession changes. However, his final pass still doesn&#x27;t consistently match the quality of the movement that gets him into dangerous areas.</p>

<h3 id="cavan-sullivan-philadelphia-union" data-draft-paragraph="31"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/cavan-sullivan.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Cavan Sullivan, Philadelphia Union</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="32">Apparently breaking records with the national team wasn&#x27;t enough for Cavan Sullivan.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="33">Fresh off becoming the youngest goalscorer in USMNT history against Mexico, Sullivan returned to Philadelphia&#x27;s starting lineup Saturday and added another two “MLS assists” as the Union defeated Real Salt Lake 2-1.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="34">The victory extended Philadelphia&#x27;s unbeaten streak to 13 MLS matches, including seven consecutive wins, and Sullivan was once again heavily involved in the attack.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="35">He helped set up an excellent team goal in the 31st minute.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="36">Sullivan received the ball on the right side of the attacking third, dribbled toward the box and drew multiple defenders in his direction. Instead of forcing his way through the crowd, he recognized the space that had opened outside and played the ball back to Frankie Westfield.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="37">Westfield immediately delivered a low cross into the penalty area. Milan Iloski cleverly allowed the ball to run through his legs, leaving Bruno Damiani with an open look that he finished to give Philadelphia the lead.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="38">Sullivan’s initial movement drew defenders toward the ball and created the space Westfield used to deliver the decisive pass.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="42">He finished with three shots and an appearance on the MLS Team of the Matchday.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="43">Sullivan now has 20 MLS-credited goal contributions this season, seven goals and 14 (MLS) assists, tying the single-season record for a teenager. His 14 assists also lead the league.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="44">Sullivan is consistently involved in the possessions that produce Philadelphia&#x27;s goals. He is finding space, drawing defenders toward him, recognizing passing lanes and increasingly making the right decisions about when to dribble and when to release the ball.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="45">Philadelphia was sitting at the bottom of the Eastern Conference when the league resumed after the World Cup. The Union have since become one of the hottest teams in MLS, and Sullivan has been a central figure in that transformation.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="46">His first senior USMNT camp only increased the excitement around him.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="47">At 17, there will still be inconsistent performances and moments when he tries something that doesn&#x27;t work. The physical and tactical challenges will also increase substantially when he eventually moves to Europe.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="48">For now, however, Sullivan is doing about everything you could reasonably ask of a teenager in MLS.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="49">The conversation has moved well beyond whether he deserves a call-up.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="50">Now it is about how quickly he can establish himself as a legitimate option for the national team.</p>

<h3 id="julian-hall-adri-mehmeti-new-york-red-bulls" data-draft-paragraph="51"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/julian-hall.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/adri-mehmeti.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Julian Hall &amp; Adri Mehmeti, New York Red Bulls</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="52">Two other young Americans returning from their first senior USMNT camp helped produce one of the weekend&#x27;s most convincing MLS victories.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="53">Julian Hall and Adri Mehmeti both started and played 90 minutes as the New York Red Bulls demolished San Diego FC 4-1 on Saturday.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="54">Hall picked up where he left off with the national team, opening the scoring in the 15th minute.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="55">Ronald Donkor advanced the ball through midfield before releasing Jorge Ruvalcaba down the right side of the penalty area. Ruvalcaba delivered a sharp, low cross across the goalmouth and Hall arrived at the back post for a simple finish.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="57">Hall has the speed and athleticism to threaten defenses in transition, but his development as a striker increasingly revolves around his movement inside the penalty area.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="58">The goal was his 11th of the MLS season, continuing an outstanding year that has seen the 18-year-old emerge as New York&#x27;s leading scorer and one of the league&#x27;s most promising young forwards. He wasn&#x27;t just a one-chance scorer Saturday, either. Hall finished with seven shots, including six on target, demonstrating how consistently he was getting into dangerous positions.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="60">Mehmeti received possession near the top of San Diego&#x27;s penalty area and played the ball across to Donkor. The midfielder immediately found Ruvalcaba making a run toward the end line, and Ruvalcaba turned before firing a shot into the upper corner from a difficult angle.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="61">It was a useful example of the qualities that make Mehmeti such an interesting midfield prospect. He recognizes passing options quickly and generally looks to move the ball before opposing defenders can close the space around him. </p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="62">There is a reason Mehmeti has become such an important part of New York&#x27;s midfield despite being only 17 years old. He is not the type of young player who overwhelms opponents with extraordinary size or speed. His strengths are more subtle: awareness before receiving, quick decision-making, intelligent positioning and the ability to connect passes through midfield.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="192">The larger concern remains his place in Monaco&#x27;s attacking hierarchy. Balogun is healthy enough to contribute again, but he has not yet reclaimed the starting striker position.</p>

<h3 id="tanner-tessmann-lyon" data-draft-paragraph="193"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/tanner-tessmann.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Tanner Tessmann, Lyon</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="194">Tessmann entered in the 77th minute of Lyon&#x27;s 2-1 defeat at Lens, replacing Tyler Morton.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="195">Lyon&#x27;s midfield struggled for much of the match, and Morton received particularly poor reviews from French coverage.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="196">That creates an interesting question about why Paulo Fonseca continues to prefer other options ahead of Tessmann.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="197">He has generally performed capably when given opportunities, but he remains outside the club&#x27;s preferred league lineup.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="198">There wasn&#x27;t enough time in this appearance to meaningfully evaluate him.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="199">His club situation is becoming more concerning because of the lack of opportunity rather than any obvious decline in his performances.</p>

<h3 id="gio-reyna-strasbourg" data-draft-paragraph="200"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/gio-reyna.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Gio Reyna, Strasbourg</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="201">Reyna was dropped to the bench Sunday as Strasbourg lost 2-1 at Nice. Possibly due to his extensive playing time for the US during the international break.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="202">Manager Hugo Oliveira made five changes to his lineup, including a substantial reshuffling of the midfield. Reyna eventually entered in the 63rd minute alongside Jacobo Ortega, and the substitutions briefly helped Strasbourg retain possession more effectively.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="203">However, the improvements did not translate into dangerous chances. Strasbourg failed to register a shot on target in the second half despite controlling a majority of the possession, and local coverage was critical of the team&#x27;s attacking performance.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="204">Reyna was not solely responsible for that. The attacking problems existed before he entered, and 27 minutes in a struggling team are not enough to prove he played poorly.</p>

<h3 id="tim-weah-marseille" data-draft-paragraph="205"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/tim-weah.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Tim Weah, Marseille</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="206">Weah missed Marseille&#x27;s 2-0 win at Troyes while serving a suspension.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="207">Marseille adjusted its lineup on the right side in his absence, but there is no reason to interpret the victory as evidence that Weah has lost his starting position.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="208">The larger concern remains his inconsistent performances earlier in the season and the disciplinary issues that have interrupted his availability.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="209">He has been trusted with a significant role by Marseille, including serving as captain, but needs to produce more convincing performances when he returns.</p>

<h2 id="eredivisie" data-draft-paragraph="210">Eredivisie</h2>

<h3 id="sergino-dest-psv" data-draft-paragraph="211"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/sergino-dest.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Sergiño Dest, PSV</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="212">Dest started and played the full 90 minutes as PSV defeated Heerenveen 2-0, earning a strong 7.6 rating from FotMob, although the broader performance was not as convincing as the final score suggests.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="213">Heerenveen created several dangerous opportunities, particularly in the first half, and PSV benefited from some poor finishing by the visitors.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="214">Dest was not directly involved in either goal, but he continued to perform his regular role at right back, providing PSV with an additional attacking outlet when the team advanced into the opposing half.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="215">His standing at the club remains extremely secure.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="216">The larger question is whether PSV can become more defensively balanced while allowing Dest and its other attacking players to push forward so aggressively.</p>

<h3 id="ricardo-pepi-psv" data-draft-paragraph="217"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/ricardo-pepi.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Ricardo Pepi, PSV</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="219">PSV has not announced a firm return date, and Peter Bosz continues to manage the striker position without the American.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="220">The timing was particularly unfortunate because Pepi had established himself as a regular starter and was scoring consistently before the setback.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="2">The final weekend before the USMNT returns to competitive action produced another collection of encouraging performances, particularly from the players who will be expected to lead the next World Cup cycle.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="3">Cavan Sullivan provided the biggest headline, scoring twice and winning a penalty as Philadelphia closed its regular season with a 4-1 victory over Toronto. The 17-year-old finished with 23 MLS goal contributions, extending his own record for a teenager and giving Mauricio Pochettino another reason to consider him for a significant role in the upcoming Nations League quarterfinals.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="4">Jack McGlynn may have had the best week of any American in Europe. The Stoke City midfielder scored in consecutive Championship victories, extending his scoring streak to three games and earning a late call-up to replace the injured Gio Reyna.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="5">Christian Pulisic also found the net, helping AC Milan rebound from a disappointing European loss with a win at Genoa. Tyler Adams scored a rare goal as Bournemouth came from behind in the Europa League, then produced a strong defensive performance in another Premier League victory.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="6">There were other notable contributions. Mark McKenzie scored for Toulouse, Tanner Tessmann recorded his first assist for Lyon, and Tim Weah captained Marseille to two impressive road victories.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="7">The biggest disappointment was Reyna&#x27;s injury. The Strasbourg midfielder scored against Toulouse but came off just three minutes later and was forced to withdraw from the national team. Zavier Gozo also suffered an injury while playing for Crystal Palace&#x27;s U21 team.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="8">With the US preparing to face Haiti in the first competitive matches of the new cycle, the club performances have become more than a weekly form check. Pochettino has brought back several established veterans while retaining many of the youngsters who impressed during September and October. Now he has to begin figuring out how all those pieces fit together.</p>

<h2 id="spotlight-players" data-draft-paragraph="19">Spotlight Players</h2>

<h3 id="cavan-sullivan-philadelphia-union" data-draft-paragraph="20"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/cavan-sullivan.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Cavan Sullivan, Philadelphia Union</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="21">Sullivan closed the MLS regular season with another standout performance in his return to the Union starting lineup.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="22">The Philadelphia teenager scored twice, won a penalty and helped create several other dangerous opportunities in the Union&#x27;s 4-1 victory over Toronto FC. The win secured sixth place in the Eastern Conference and a first-round playoff matchup against New England.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="23">In the 11th minute, Sullivan contributed a clever backheel during a flowing attacking sequence that ended with older brother Quinn Sullivan putting the ball in the net. Unfortunately for Philadelphia, Quinn was offside and the goal was disallowed.</p>

Vassilev then found Cavan arriving in front of goal, and he finished comfortably with his left foot.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="25">It wasn&#x27;t a spectacular individual effort, but it was another example of Sullivan&#x27;s improving movement without the ball. He recognized how the play was developing and put himself in a position where a relatively simple pass produced a goal.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="30">It was the first primary assist Quinn has provided for his younger brother in MLS, which was a nice additional moment for the family.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="31">Sullivan finished with five shots, three on target and two goals. Philadelphia generated 19 shots despite having less possession than Toronto, frequently finding opportunities to attack quickly before the visitors could organize their defense.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="32">That approach suited Sullivan perfectly. He is increasingly dangerous whether he receives the ball in space and attacks a defender himself or makes a run to finish a move created by someone else.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="33">The final numbers are remarkable.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="34">Sullivan finished the regular season with nine goals and 14 MLS-credited assists, giving him 23 goal contributions, the most by any teenager in league history. MLS&#x27;s assist accounting includes secondary assists, but even with that distinction, the production is exceptional.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="35">Perhaps more impressive is how quickly everything changed.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="36">Before August, Sullivan had one goal and four assists. Since then, he has been one of the most productive attackers in the league, helping transform Philadelphia from a struggling team into one of the hottest clubs in MLS.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="37">Interim coach Ryan Richter deserves some credit for recognizing that Sullivan was ready for a much larger role. The increased playing time gave him the opportunity to develop confidence against senior competition rather than merely accumulate occasional substitute appearances.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="38">Sullivan took a knock late in Saturday&#x27;s game and was substituted near the end, although Richter indicated afterward that he did not believe the injury was particularly serious.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="39">Assuming he is healthy, Sullivan will now join Pochettino&#x27;s squad for the Nations League quarterfinals against Haiti.</p>

<h3 id="christian-pulisic-ac-milan" data-draft-paragraph="40"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/christian-pulisic.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Christian Pulisic, AC Milan</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="41">Pulisic&#x27;s return to his best form continues to progress, although this was a week of contrasting performances. Milan began with a disappointing 2-0 Europa League loss to Hungarian side Ferencváros. Pulisic started on the bench before Rúben Amorim introduced him at halftime as part of three changes with the match still scoreless.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="42">Unfortunately, he did not provide the same attacking spark he has delivered in several previous substitute appearances.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="43">Pulisic completed 30 of his 32 passes and generally kept possession moving, but his involvement was rarely particularly threatening. He managed one shot off target and struggled to create openings against a Ferencváros defense that became increasingly comfortable protecting its lead.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="44">Sunday&#x27;s match at Genoa was a different story, as Pulisic returned to the starting lineup, operating underneath striker Gonçalo Ramos, contributing to two early goals as Milan built a 2-0 lead inside the first 20 minutes.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="45">Pulisic set up the first goal with a great defensive effort. Dispossessing the Genoa defender in the press to key a break that ended with a Cisse goal.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="47">It was his only shot of the game, but it demonstrated his ability to recognize when to move into a dangerous position and arrive there before the defense can respond. Playing near Ramos allows him to make those runs into the box while still dropping into midfield to help connect possession.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="48">Pulisic won three of four tackles in the game and completed 37 of 39 passes. He received a yellow card late in the second half before departing in the 79th minute.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="49">Italian reviews were positive, with Gazzetta dello Sport and SempreMilan both giving him a 7/10. The latter also noted that he was not always comfortable receiving with his back to goal.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="50">The important thing from a US perspective is that Pulisic is consistently starting again and producing goals. Earlier in the season, there were legitimate questions about how quickly he would work his way back into Amorim&#x27;s team after missing most of the preseason. That concern appears to be fading.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="51">For the USMNT, having Pulisic available for the Haiti series is another encouraging sign. He will be joining a very different group from the one that played the World Cup this summer, and the possibility of combining him with players such as Sullivan and Balogun gives Pochettino some intriguing attacking options.</p>

<h3 id="jack-mcglynn-stoke-city" data-draft-paragraph="52"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/jack-mcglynn.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Jack McGlynn, Stoke City</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="53">There may not be an American player whose club situation has improved more dramatically this fall than Jack McGlynn.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="54">After arriving at Stoke City during the summer, the former Philadelphia Union midfielder has become one of the most productive players in the Championship. This week he scored in consecutive victories over Blackburn and Portsmouth, extending his scoring streak to three games.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="55">The two goals showed different parts of his game.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="56">On Tuesday at Blackburn, McGlynn scored an audacious free kick in the 43rd minute. Positioned wide on the left in an area where everyone expected a cross, McGlynn recognized that goalkeeper Balázs Tóth was vulnerable and opened his body as though preparing a delivery into the penalty area.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="57">Instead, he went directly for goal.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="58">Tóth managed to get a hand on the ball, but goal-line technology confirmed that it had crossed into the net.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="59">Stoke manager Mark Robins was particularly impressed by McGlynn&#x27;s recognition of the opportunity and his willingness to attempt a shot most players would never consider.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="60">Technique has never really been in question with McGlynn. His left foot is among the best in the American player pool, particularly when he has time to pick a pass or deliver a set piece. The more interesting development is how Stoke is finding different ways to use that ability.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="61">Against Blackburn, McGlynn played centrally, completing 39 of 50 passes and creating two additional shooting opportunities. He was involved in the buildup and distribution while still finding a moment of individual quality to change the game.</p>

In the fourth minute, Marvin Schwäbe denied him from close range during a sequence that also included attempts from Patrik Schick and Maza. Later, he went narrowly wide from about 20 meters.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="198">Patrick Scherer of <em>Kölner Stadt-Anzeiger</em> gave him a 3 on the German scale, where 1 is best, citing the intensity of his duels, his defensive work near his own box and a pass that created a big chance for Maza.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="199">The missed early chance and the pass to Maza sum up the case for Tillman. When he gets regular time between the lines, he creates danger for himself and for teammates. The challenge is still getting that time consistently at Leverkusen. </p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="200">Still, he remains involved at a high level and will be an important attacking midfield option for Pochettino against Haiti.</p>

<h3 id="joe-scally-borussia-monchengladbach" data-draft-paragraph="201"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/joe-scally.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Joe Scally, Borussia Mönchengladbach</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="202">Scally&#x27;s week captured both sides of his new attacking role in one match.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="203">In Friday&#x27;s 3-1 loss at Hamburg, he got Gladbach back level in the 44th minute with a bouncing effort that took a touch off Robin Hack on its way in. The Bundesliga and the German federation credit Scally with the goal, while Opta and FBref give it to Hack with Scally earning the assist. Either way, he created it.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="204">He nearly scored again when Shuto Machino set him up, but Sebastiaan Bornauw recovered to block the shot. <em>GladbachLive</em> gave him a 3 on the German scale, praising the technique on the goal while noting some sloppy simple passes going forward.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="205">The downside came early. In the eighth minute, Scally headed the ball straight into Patson Daka&#x27;s path, and goalkeeper Daniel Batz had to make a one-on-one save.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="206">Ko Itakura&#x27;s second yellow card after halftime changed Gladbach&#x27;s defensive situation considerably, and Hamburg&#x27;s later long-range goals shouldn&#x27;t be pinned on Scally.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="207">He continues to get forward far more often than he did earlier in his career, with three shots in this match. The early back-header is the type of mistake he has to eliminate. </p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="208">He remains outside the USMNT squad for Haiti.</p>

<h3 id="mathis-albert-borussia-dortmund" data-draft-paragraph="209"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/mathis-albert.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Mathis Albert, Borussia Dortmund</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="210">Albert was an unused substitute in both of Dortmund&#x27;s victories this week, against Real Betis in the Champions League and Elversberg in the Bundesliga.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="211">The fact that he is regularly around the first team remains encouraging for a 17-year-old, although he has not yet turned those bench appearances into meaningful playing time.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="212">Dortmund also did not use him in the corresponding Youth League or reserve fixtures.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="213">Albert will join Pochettino&#x27;s squad for the Haiti matches after making his senior national-team debut earlier this fall.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="214">His immediate club situation remains a developmental one. Training with elite players and earning selection to the first-team bench is useful, but he will eventually need regular competitive minutes to continue progressing.</p>

<h3 id="cole-campbell-elversberg" data-draft-paragraph="215"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/cole-campbell.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Cole Campbell, Elversberg</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="216">Campbell had a lively cameo against his former club, replacing Francis Onyeka in the 67th minute of Saturday&#x27;s 2-1 loss at Borussia Dortmund.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="217">In about 23 minutes, he attempted two shots, putting one on target, and completed 10 of 13 passes. That is more than a token contribution for a substitute, although he is still working toward regular starts. </p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="218">Cherundolo has named him to the under-23 squad for the Spain camp.</p>

<h3 id="lennard-maloney-mainz" data-draft-paragraph="219">Lennard Maloney, Mainz</h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="220">Maloney was an unused substitute in Mainz&#x27;s 2-1 defeat to Bayern Munich.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="221">He has received only limited playing time in recent weeks and remains outside the club&#x27;s preferred starting midfield.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="222">There was no confirmed injury or suspension preventing him from playing Saturday.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="223">The situation is becoming increasingly frustrating because Maloney&#x27;s physical and defensive qualities should be useful in a Bundesliga midfield.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="224">However, he needs to earn a more substantial club role before he can realistically return to the USMNT conversation.</p>

<h3 id="santiago-castaneda-sc-paderborn" data-draft-paragraph="225">Santiago Castañeda, SC Paderborn</h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="226">A new name on the list, Castañeda continued his impressive start to life in the Bundesliga, playing another full 90 minutes as newly promoted Paderborn drew 2-2 with Eintracht Frankfurt on Saturday.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="227">The 21-year-old American operated in central midfield and was an important part of a Paderborn team that controlled 61 percent of possession against a much more established Bundesliga opponent.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="228">Castañeda nearly created the opening goal in the 13th minute, receiving the ball inside the Frankfurt penalty area before finding Ruben Müller at the back post. Unfortunately, Müller couldn&#x27;t quite reach the pass for what would have been a simple finish.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="229">Early in the second half, Castañeda delivered another dangerous ball into the box that Müller narrowly missed from close range. A minute later, the American tried his luck from distance but sent his shot wide.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="230">He finished with 43 of 51 passes completed, two chances created and three interceptions. His influence was not limited to defensive work, as he regularly advanced into positions where he could contribute to Paderborn&#x27;s attack.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="231">Paderborn appeared headed for an impressive victory after taking a 2-1 lead against ten-man Frankfurt, only to concede a heartbreaking equalizer in the third minute of stoppage time.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="232">The bigger story is Castañeda&#x27;s emergence as a dependable Bundesliga starter. He has now played every minute of Paderborn&#x27;s first nine league matches, an impressive achievement for a young midfielder making his debut season in Germany&#x27;s top division.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="233">That consistency has earned him his first senior USMNT call-up for the upcoming Nations League matches against Haiti.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="234">Castañeda offers an interesting combination of size, defensive range and passing ability in central midfield. He is not necessarily the creative midfielder the US has sometimes lacked, but he is demonstrating that he can handle the physical demands and speed of play in the Bundesliga.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="235">After spending the past several years largely outside the national-team conversation, he has suddenly become one of the more interesting young options in the American midfield pool.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="236">Pochettino will now get an opportunity to evaluate him up close.</p>

<h2 id="ligue-1" data-draft-paragraph="237">Ligue 1</h2>

<h3 id="gio-reyna-strasbourg" data-draft-paragraph="238">Gio Reyna, Strasbourg</h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="239">Reyna&#x27;s week produced one of the more frustrating combinations possible for a USMNT player: another encouraging goal followed almost immediately by an injury.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="240">He started centrally in Strasbourg&#x27;s 3-1 defeat at Toulouse and scored in the 67th minute to briefly bring his team back into the game.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="241">The goal came from an unusual sequence.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="242">Reyna initially combined with teammate Jacobo Ortega near the top of the penalty area but lost possession. Toulouse attempted to play out, only for Thomas Jørgensen to send an inaccurate pass through Mark McKenzie&#x27;s legs and back into Reyna&#x27;s path.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="243">Reyna reacted quickly and finished with his right foot.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="244">It was a fortunate opportunity created by a defensive mistake, but Reyna deserves credit for staying involved after losing the initial possession. He finished with two shots, one on target, and completed 31 of 40 passes.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="245">Unfortunately, he went down injured shortly afterward and was substituted in the 70th minute.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="246">French reporting suggested a possible thigh problem, but there has been no confirmed diagnosis or recovery timetable. U.S. Soccer subsequently announced that Reyna would withdraw from the Haiti squad, with McGlynn taking his place.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="247">The injury is especially disappointing because Reyna had begun establishing some positive momentum at Strasbourg.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="248">He has now scored three league goals this season and has been receiving more consistent opportunities than he did during several injury-disrupted years.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="250">Unfortunately, that remains the central concern.</p>

<h3 id="mark-mckenzie-toulouse" data-draft-paragraph="251"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/mark-mckenzie.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Mark McKenzie, Toulouse</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="252">McKenzie had a much better result than Reyna in their head-to-head matchup, scoring in Toulouse&#x27;s 3-1 victory over Strasbourg.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="253">The American center back found the net in the 56th minute, attacking an inswinging corner from Cristian Cásseres Jr. and heading home from close range.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="254">It was an excellent example of McKenzie&#x27;s ability to contribute on set pieces.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="255">He was also efficient in possession, completing 72 of 76 passes and helping Toulouse maintain control through much of the game. MadeInFOOT gave him a 7/10.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="256">Overall, it was a strong performance and another positive development for an American defender who continues playing regularly in France.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="257">McKenzie was also added to the USMNT squad this week after George Campbell withdrew through injury.</p>

<h3 id="tim-weah-marseille" data-draft-paragraph="258">Tim Weah, Marseille</h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="259">Weah enjoyed one of his more encouraging weeks of the season, captaining Marseille to two impressive road victories.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="260">In Thursday&#x27;s 1-0 Europa League victory at Bayer Leverkusen, his main job was supporting Kelyann Bezahaf and preventing Leverkusen from getting around Marseille&#x27;s right side. <em>Le Phocéen</em> gave him a 6/10. He completed 26 of 30 passes, a low volume that reflects how defensive that assignment was, and Marseille&#x27;s right side held up when Leverkusen pressed late for an equalizer.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="261">At Lens on Sunday, Weah played right back in a 2-1 win and completed 62 of 65 passes. <em>FootMercato</em> gave him a 5.5, noting that he won all six of his duels but finding his final delivery too imprecise.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="262">That was visible in the match. An early ball over the top got Himad Abdelli behind the defense but too wide to keep the attack alive, and a later cross led only to an unthreatening bicycle-kick attempt from Igor Paixão. He also drew a booking on Yacine Titraoui and a free kick near the box.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="263">Weah&#x27;s ability to cover two very different jobs on the same flank is a big part of his value to Marseille. </p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="264">He was still left out of the Haiti squad.</p>

<h3 id="tanner-tessmann-lyon" data-draft-paragraph="265"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/tanner-tessmann.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Tanner Tessmann, Lyon</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="266">Tessmann produced one of his better European performances Thursday, providing an assist in Lyon&#x27;s 1-1 Europa League draw at Real Sociedad.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="267">Playing from the base of midfield, Tessmann found Corentin Tolisso with an excellent ball into the penalty area in the 54th minute. Tolisso controlled the pass on his chest before finishing with a left-footed volley.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="268">Remarkably, it was Tessmann&#x27;s first recorded assist in 92 appearances for Lyon.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="269">The pass was a good example of what Tessmann can provide when he has time and space to distribute from deep positions. He has the range to recognize runners ahead of him and deliver passes that bypass several defenders rather than simply keeping possession moving sideways.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="270">Tessmann also contributed defensively and received a 6.5/10 from Maxifoot.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="271">Sunday&#x27;s 2-0 defeat at Brest was less successful for Lyon, although Tessmann&#x27;s individual performance was not necessarily poor. He completed 69 of 78 passes and won five of six tackles. Those are useful numbers, particularly for a defensive midfielder, but the broader question is how much they helped Lyon generate attacking danger.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="272">Brest defended in a compact shape, and Lyon struggled to create quality opportunities.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="273">French evaluations of Tessmann varied substantially, with some viewing him as one of Lyon&#x27;s better players and others criticizing his inability to change the game offensively.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="274">Tessmann performed many of his individual responsibilities effectively, but controlling possession from deep positions is not the same as breaking down an organized defense.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="275">His assist against Sociedad showed the attacking value he can add when he identifies the right opportunity.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="276">Pochettino left him out of the Haiti roster, but another stretch of regular starts and performances like Thursday&#x27;s will keep him in the midfield discussion.</p>

<h3 id="bryan-reynolds-rennes" data-draft-paragraph="277"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/bryan-reynolds.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Bryan Reynolds, Rennes</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="278">Reynolds played two full matches in two different positions and received two very different reviews.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="279">In Wednesday&#x27;s 0-0 Europa League draw with Olympiacos, he started at right back. He hit a first-time shot from the corner of the area just wide, set up Mousa Al-Tamari for a saved effort from a tight angle and twice played balls in behind that ended in offside calls. <em>Homme du Match</em> gave him a 6.5/10, praising his dribbling and willingness to defend.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="280">Against Lille on Sunday, he played higher on the right in a 2-1 loss. His involvement increased, with 40 of 49 passes completed and two shots blocked, but <em>Homme du Match</em> gave him a 4, finding his influence on Rennes&#x27; buildup limited despite the extra touches.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="281">The positional flexibility is useful, and Pochettino now has a player in camp who has just played full matches as both a fullback and a wide midfielder. But the weekend raised a fair question about how much of that extra possession turned into productive entries into the final third.</p>

<h3 id="folarin-balogun-monaco" data-draft-paragraph="283"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/folarin-balogun.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Folarin Balogun, Monaco</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="284">Balogun&#x27;s return to regular playing time continues, but his attacking production remains inconsistent.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="285">He started Thursday&#x27;s 2-0 Conference League victory over Nordsjælland, operating near the front alongside Paris Brunner. Balogun struggled to become involved in Monaco&#x27;s attacking combinations, touching the ball infrequently and failing to consistently create separation from defenders.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="286">His best opportunity came in the 59th minute when Vanderson delivered a cross from the right and Balogun met it with a close-range header, only for goalkeeper Andreas Hansen to make an excellent save. It was a chance he will be disappointed not to convert, although the goalkeeper deserves credit.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="287">Sunday at Le Mans was even quieter.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="288">Balogun entered in the 54th minute and played the final 36 minutes of Monaco&#x27;s 1-1 draw. He attempted no shots and completed only four of five passes. There was very little involvement, either as a target in buildup or a runner threatening the defense.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="289">To be fair, the two appearances came in different circumstances, and Monaco did not consistently provide the type of service that allows Balogun to attack space behind defenders.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="290">Still, the lack of touches and chances is concerning.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="291">Balogun&#x27;s movement and finishing remain among the best qualities in the American striker pool, but he needs to become more influential when his team cannot consistently create opportunities for him.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="292">He will join the USMNT for the Haiti matches, where Pochettino will hope to get him operating much more effectively alongside the team&#x27;s attacking midfielders.</p>

<h2 id="eredivisie" data-draft-paragraph="293">Eredivisie</h2>

<h3 id="sergino-dest-psv-eindhoven" data-draft-paragraph="294"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/sergino-dest.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Sergiño Dest, PSV Eindhoven</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="295">Dest was PSV&#x27;s most dangerous player in Wednesday&#x27;s 2-1 Champions League loss to Club Brugge.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="296">Playing right back, he repeatedly got behind Brugge&#x27;s defense and finished with five shots, three on target, according to FBref. He forced an early save, created another opening for himself and produced a slaloming run through the defense that ended with a shot just wide.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="297"><em>VoetbalPrimeur</em> gave him a 6/10, calling him PSV&#x27;s most dangerous player before halftime, but also argued that on his first chance he should have squared the ball to Ruben van Bommel.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="298">He played 75 minutes in Sunday&#x27;s 5-2 win at Cambuur, receiving a yellow card in the 72nd minute before Kiliann Sildillia replaced him. Sven Mijnans scored a hat trick. Dest goes into the Haiti camp as an established starter who keeps creating danger from right back.</p>

<h3 id="ricardo-pepi-psv-eindhoven" data-draft-paragraph="299"><span class="article-spotlight-name"><img class="article-spotlight-avatar" src="{{ '/assets/images/player-pool/ricardo-pepi.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" loading="lazy">Ricardo Pepi, PSV Eindhoven</span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="300">Pepi appeared in both PSV games this week, but neither offered much opportunity to change the result.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="301">He played the final 21 minutes against Club Brugge, recording two shots without scoring. One attempt went over the goal and another was blocked.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="302">Sunday at Cambuur, Pepi entered in the 64th minute and played the final 26 minutes of PSV&#x27;s 5-2 victory. He again had a shot blocked but otherwise produced limited attacking involvement.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="303">Manager Peter Bosz indicated before the Brugge match that Pepi was healthy enough to start following his recent return from injury. However, Bosz selected Sven Mijnans centrally against Brugge and Guus Til against Cambuur.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="304">That suggests Pepi&#x27;s limited minutes were not simply the result of a medical restriction. He is once again facing real competition for the starting striker position.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="305">For the USMNT, the situation is worth monitoring. Pepi has demonstrated that he can score consistently for PSV, but he needs to reclaim regular starts to maintain that momentum.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="306">Pochettino left him at PSV during the international break, giving him an uninterrupted period to work with his club.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="2">The Champions League returns this week with a considerably more open race for Europe&#x27;s top prize than we had a month ago. PSG remains atop our European Power Rankings, but the two-time defending champions have now lost both Khvicha Kvaratskhelia and Désiré Doué to serious knee injuries. Meanwhile, reigning Ballon d&#x27;Or winner Harry Kane will miss several months after undergoing ankle surgery, dropping Bayern Munich out of our top tier.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="3">Barcelona, Real Madrid and Arsenal have all put together excellent starts to their domestic campaigns and should see an opportunity to dethrone the French champions.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="4">Perhaps the most interesting development further down the rankings is the strength at the top of Serie A. Six Italian teams make our top 20, with just six points separating the top five in the league. In England, Brighton, Bournemouth and Brentford continue to outperform several of the traditional powers, while Chelsea, Napoli and Manchester United have all fallen out of our top 20.</p>

<h2 class="power-rankings-tier-heading power-rankings-tier-heading--1 power-rankings-tier-heading--nov24" id="tier-1" data-draft-paragraph="5"><strong>Tier 1: The Favorites</strong></h2>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-1"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/psg.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="6"><strong>1. PSG (Previous: 1)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="7">PSG has done nothing on the field to warrant dropping them from the top spot. The French champions have won all four of their Champions League games, scoring 20 goals in the process, with impressive victories over Manchester City and Barcelona followed by a 6-0 demolition of Villarreal.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="8">However, the PSG team that produced those results is not the same team we will see for the remainder of the season. Kvaratskhelia and Doué have both suffered ACL tears in the past five weeks and are likely out for the season.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="9">One of the greatest strengths of Luis Enrique&#x27;s team has been the interchangeability of its attackers and the number of players capable of beating a defender individually. Losing two of those players will make PSG considerably easier to defend and places a greater burden on Ousmane Dembélé.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="10">The outstanding midfield trio of Vitinha, João Neves and Fabián Ruiz remains intact, and PSG has enough attacking depth to remain the top team for now. However, the separation between them and the rest of Europe has shrunk considerably.</p>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-2"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/barcelona.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="11"><strong>2. Barcelona (Previous: 2)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="12">Barcelona&#x27;s attacking production this season has been absurd. Through 13 La Liga games, Hansi Flick&#x27;s team has scored 51 goals, averaging nearly four per game while winning all but one.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="13">The decision to move forward without a traditional world-class center forward continues to look like the right one. The movement of Raphinha and the collection of talented attacking midfielders and wingers has made Barcelona difficult to defend without sacrificing much of the finishing production Robert Lewandowski previously provided.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="14">The defensive vulnerabilities that come with Flick&#x27;s aggressive approach remain, as demonstrated in the 4-2 loss to PSG last month and Sunday&#x27;s 3-2 win over Villarreal. The biggest question is whether Barcelona can maintain enough control against the handful of teams with the technical ability and speed to consistently exploit their high defensive line.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="15">With PSG&#x27;s injuries, Barcelona has a very compelling argument as the favorite to win the Champions League.</p>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-3"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/real-madrid.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="16"><strong>3. Real Madrid (Previous: 4)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="17">Real Madrid is one of two teams with a perfect record in the Champions League and sits just three points behind Barcelona in La Liga.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="18">José Mourinho&#x27;s team hasn&#x27;t been nearly as overwhelming as Barcelona domestically, but they have been effective, and there is plenty of reason to believe their approach will translate well to the Champions League knockout rounds.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="19">The combination of Kylian Mbappé, Vinícius Jr. and Madrid&#x27;s other attacking options makes them particularly dangerous against opponents willing to commit numbers forward. The concern remains how consistently Madrid can control games when their opponents take away those transition opportunities.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="20">The Champions League showdown with Arsenal in December should provide one of the better measuring sticks of the first half of the season.</p>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-4"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/arsenal.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="21"><strong>4. Arsenal (Previous: 5)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="22">Arsenal has established itself as the clear class of the Premier League this season, opening up a six-point lead over Brighton and Manchester City while remaining unbeaten in the Champions League.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="23">Of the four teams in this tier, Arsenal may have the fewest obvious tactical vulnerabilities. They can control possession, defend comfortably for extended stretches and generate goals from set pieces when their attack struggles to break down an opponent.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="24">The other three teams probably have a higher attacking ceiling, but Arsenal&#x27;s balance and defensive structure give them a better chance of consistently producing results even when their most talented attackers aren&#x27;t at their best.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="25">Mikel Arteta&#x27;s team has reached the point where anything short of seriously contending for both the Premier League and Champions League titles would be a disappointment.</p>

<h2 class="power-rankings-tier-heading power-rankings-tier-heading--2 power-rankings-tier-heading--nov24" id="tier-2" data-draft-paragraph="26"><strong>Tier 2: The Challengers</strong></h2>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-5"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/bayern-munich.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="27"><strong>5. Bayern Munich (Previous: 3)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="28">Bayern would still be in our top tier if not for the injury to Harry Kane, who is expected to miss several months after undergoing ankle surgery.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="29">Kane is particularly difficult to replace because he provides so much more than goals. His ability to drop into midfield, hold possession and find the runs of Michael Olise and Bayern&#x27;s other attackers is a crucial component of their offensive structure. Replacing him with a more conventional striker could fundamentally change the way Bayern attacks.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="30">Even before the injury, Bayern had been dropping more points domestically than expected, drawing four of its first ten Bundesliga games. They remain enormously talented, but the next few months should tell us how much of their offensive success is attributable to Kane.</p>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-6"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/manchester-city.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="31"><strong>6. Manchester City (Previous: 6)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="32">The transition from Pep Guardiola to Enzo Maresca has produced somewhat uneven results. City has been more successful in the Champions League than domestically of late, with the 3-2 win at Leipzig providing a positive result against an improving opponent.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="33">The talent on this roster remains sufficient to win either major competition, especially with Erling Haaland leading the attack. However, City hasn&#x27;t consistently demonstrated the ability to control games and prevent opponents from creating dangerous moments in transition the way Guardiola&#x27;s best teams did.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="34">The upcoming games against Arsenal and Barcelona should tell us quite a bit about whether City belongs back in the top tier.</p>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-7"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/inter-milan.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="35"><strong>7. Inter Milan (Previous: 7)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="36">Inter remains one of the most talented and experienced teams in Europe, but there have been some concerning defensive performances this season.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="37">The 3-1 derby loss to AC Milan was followed a few weeks later by a wild 4-4 draw with Atalanta. Inter has conceded 17 goals through 12 Serie A games, considerably more than its primary title rivals.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="38">Their attacking production has been enough to compensate for much of that domestically, but the defensive solidity that has underpinned their recent European success will need to return if they want to make another deep Champions League run.</p>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-8"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/borussia-dortmund.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="39"><strong>8. Borussia Dortmund (Previous: 11)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="40">Dortmund has quietly overtaken Bayern at the top of the Bundesliga and remains unbeaten domestically, conceding just seven goals through ten games.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="41">They don&#x27;t possess nearly the same attacking firepower as Bayern, and their loss at Bodø/Glimt in the Champions League was a reminder that this is not yet an elite team. However, Dortmund&#x27;s defensive consistency makes them a particularly difficult opponent.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="42">With Bayern now missing Kane, there is a very real opportunity for Dortmund to win its first Bundesliga title since 2012. Today&#x27;s game at Arsenal will provide a good indication of how seriously we should take their Champions League aspirations as well.</p>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-9"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/roma.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="43"><strong>9. Roma (Previous: 10)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="44">Gian Piero Gasperini&#x27;s team remains unbeaten in Serie A and has scored a league-leading 36 goals through 12 games. Donyell Malen continued his outstanding season with four goals in Saturday&#x27;s 5-1 win over Parma.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="45">Gasperini appears to have found a collection of attacking players well suited to his approach, with Malen&#x27;s movement and ability to attack space complementing the more creative players around him.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="46">The domestic success hasn&#x27;t entirely translated to the Champions League, where Roma has just four points through four games. However, they played Real Madrid competitively in a 4-3 loss and will get another opportunity to prove themselves against PSG this week.</p>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-10"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/lazio.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="47"><strong>10. Lazio (Previous: 16)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="48">At some point we have to stop treating Lazio&#x27;s start as a surprise and recognize them as legitimate Serie A title contenders.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="49">Lazio has won ten of its first 12 league games without losing, conceding just nine goals. They have been more consistent domestically than any of the more heralded Italian teams.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="50">The lack of a Champions League measuring stick makes them a little more difficult to place than Roma or Inter, but nearly a third of the way through the Italian season, their position atop the table is well earned.</p>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-11"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/atletico-madrid.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="51"><strong>11. Atlético Madrid (Previous: 8)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="52">Atlético has put together an excellent domestic campaign, sitting just five points behind Barcelona and two behind Real Madrid.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="53">Unfortunately, they haven&#x27;t been nearly as successful in their biggest European games, losing to Liverpool and Bayern and collecting just four points through four Champions League matches.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="54">Diego Simeone has a talented and deep roster, and the defensive numbers in La Liga suggest Atlético remains a difficult team to break down. However, they haven&#x27;t demonstrated that they can consistently generate enough offense against Europe&#x27;s best teams.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="55">There is probably more upside to this group than we have seen so far.</p>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-12"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/liverpool.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="56"><strong>12. Liverpool (Previous: 9)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="57">Liverpool returned from the international break with an emphatic 4-0 victory over Manchester United, although given United&#x27;s current state, that result may not carry the weight it normally would.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="58">Andoni Iraola&#x27;s team has shown plenty of attacking potential, but their inconsistency has kept them from mounting a more serious challenge to Arsenal domestically. The 3-2 loss at Fenerbahçe also exposed some of their defensive vulnerabilities.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="59">Liverpool has enough talent to compete with anyone in the rankings, but they need to become better at controlling games rather than relying on their attacking players to bail them out when matches become stretched.</p>

<h2 class="power-rankings-tier-heading power-rankings-tier-heading--3 power-rankings-tier-heading--nov24" id="tier-3" data-draft-paragraph="60"><strong>Tier 3: The Emerging Contenders</strong></h2>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-13"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/ac-milan.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="61"><strong>13. AC Milan (Previous: 23)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="62">Milan makes a significant jump into our top 20 after winning three consecutive Serie A games, including an impressive 3-1 victory over Inter. </p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="63">Rúben Amorim&#x27;s team has lost just once domestically and has gradually worked its way into a crowded Italian title race. Having a healthy Christian Pulisic available for a more substantial role has also given Milan another dangerous attacking option.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="64">The biggest reason they aren&#x27;t higher is their inexplicably poor Europa League campaign. Milan has just three points through four games, most recently losing 2-0 at home to Ferencváros. Their domestic performances suggest they are capable of considerably more.</p>

<figure class="soccer-article-figure soccer-article-figure--uncropped"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/usmnt-weekly-2026-11-24/christian-pulisic-frosinone.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Christian Pulisic carries the ball against Frosinone" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Christian Pulisic in action during Milan’s win over Frosinone. Photo: <a href="https://www.acmilan.com/it/news/fotogallery/serie-a/2026-11-22/milan-frosinone-serie-a-2026-2027" target="_blank" rel="noopener">AC Milan</a>.</figcaption></figure>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-14"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/como.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="65"><strong>14. Como (Previous: 19)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="66">The best story in European soccer this season continues to be Cesc Fàbregas&#x27; Como team.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="67">Playing in Europe for the first time in club history, Como has scored 19 goals through four Champions League games, including a ridiculous 9-1 victory at Lens in the previous round.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="68">The most impressive part of Como&#x27;s emergence is that they haven&#x27;t needed to adopt an underdog mentality against more established clubs. Fàbregas has built a technically proficient team that is comfortable playing through pressure and taking the game to its opponents.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="69">They are also just six points off the lead in Serie A. The upcoming Champions League games against PSG and Barcelona will be fascinating tests of how far this team has come.</p>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-15"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/brighton.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="70"><strong>15. Brighton (Previous: 14)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="71">Despite a disappointing 2-0 loss at Hull City this weekend, Brighton remains second in the Premier League with the best goal differential in England.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="72">Brighton has consistently identified and developed talented players, but this season it appears they have assembled a group capable of achieving more than simply producing the next lucrative transfer.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="73">The question is whether they can maintain their level over a full Premier League season. Based on their performances through the first 11 games, a Champions League berth is a perfectly reasonable aspiration.</p>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-16"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/bournemouth.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="74"><strong>16. Bournemouth (Previous: 18)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="75">Bournemouth has recovered spectacularly from a winless opening six Premier League games, winning five consecutive league matches and nine straight across all competitions under Marco Rose. Both streaks represent club records, and the Cherries have climbed into fifth place. </p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="76">Their ability to compete domestically while also adjusting to the demands of European soccer is encouraging. Bournemouth&#x27;s aggressive approach without the ball makes them a difficult matchup even for teams with considerably more talented rosters.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="77">The next step is becoming more consistent at turning competitive performances into wins. They have already shown they can trouble the best teams in England.</p>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-17"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/brentford.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="78"><strong>17. Brentford (Previous: 15)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="79">Brentford put an exclamation point on the weekend with Monday&#x27;s 4-0 victory over Everton, moving into sixth place in the Premier League.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="80">Perhaps the most impressive aspect of Brentford&#x27;s continued success is how well the club has adapted to the departures of important players and coaches over the past few seasons. There remains a clear identity to the team regardless of the personnel.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="81">Their plus-nine goal differential is actually better than Manchester City&#x27;s, suggesting their position in the standings is not merely the product of a few fortunate results.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="82">Brighton, Bournemouth and Brentford occupying three of the top six spots in the Premier League is one of the more interesting developments of the season.</p>

<h2 class="power-rankings-tier-heading power-rankings-tier-heading--4 power-rankings-tier-heading--nov24" id="tier-4" data-draft-paragraph="83"><strong>Tier 4: Teams on the Rise</strong></h2>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-18"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/porto.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="84"><strong>18. Porto (Previous: 26)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="85">Eleven games, eleven wins.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="86">Porto has been thoroughly dominant in Portugal this season, scoring 27 goals and conceding just five. However, their first three Champions League games all ended in defeat, raising some questions about how much of that dominance was attributable to the level of domestic competition.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="87">A 4-1 victory over Napoli in the previous Champions League round provided a much more convincing indication of Porto&#x27;s quality. If they can start reproducing their domestic form in Europe, they could climb another tier fairly quickly.</p>

<h3 class="power-rankings-team-heading power-rankings-team-heading--nov24" id="rank-19"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/club-logos/juventus.png' | relative_url }}" alt="" width="50" height="50" loading="lazy"><span class="power-rankings-team-heading__name" data-draft-paragraph="88"><strong>19. Juventus (Previous: 21)</strong></span></h3>

<p data-draft-paragraph="89">Juventus has shown some signs of life in recent weeks, including a victory over Napoli and a 4-0 win over Venezia this weekend.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="90">The problem for Juventus is that the top of Serie A has become considerably more competitive. They have lost three of their first 12 league games and already sit 11 points behind Lazio.</p>

<p data-draft-paragraph="91">Luciano Spalletti has a talented enough roster to close that gap, but Juventus needs to start producing the sort of convincing attacking performances we saw this weekend with much greater frequency.</p>

What this measures

DEPI is the Dual Eights predictive power rating, expressed in points relative to an average NFL team. It blends opponent-adjusted offense, defense and special teams with the expected quarterback and carries a share of prior-season strength. The difference between two teams' ratings, plus home field ({{ nfl.home_advantage | round: 1 }} points), is the model's projected margin. Unlike the Efficiency Index, it is built to forecast games rather than describe them. Higher is better. All 32 teams →

Why Dual Eights?

In soccer, “dual eights” are two all-action central midfielders who connect defense and attack. In Baltimore, the number links the city’s sporting past and present: Cal Ripken Jr. set the standard, and Lamar Jackson carries it forward. This site lives where those traditions meet.

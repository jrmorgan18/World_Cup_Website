Research flags, not automatic downgrades. Review players, replacements and returns, then use Review / adjust to enter a positive or negative team adjustment. It affects upcoming spreads; rankings change only when you select that option. Your scenarios stay in this browser session.

Who qualifies: at least two meaningful players in the same group must be confirmed OUT / IR, or have two consecutive injury-related DNP reports in the game week. A later limited or full practice removes the DNP flag. Questionable, doubtful, a single DNP and routine limited practice do not qualify on their own. Returning to practice does not clear an OUT designation or activate a player from IR.

All-team report coverage

The latest-status feed does not establish daily DNP history or complete IR coverage. Consecutive DNPs and IR require dated, sourced evidence.

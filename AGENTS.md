# Article TLDR standard

Every new article must include a `tldr` list in its front matter. Use three to five short bullets, roughly 100 words total, to summarize the key conclusions and supporting facts in plain language.

The standard format is collapsed by default with a clickable TLDR label. The post layout places it below the header photo and above the article text.

Use the TLDR in `_posts/2026-11-06-ravens-first-and-five-jaguars-week-nine.md` as the reference for the amount of detail. Supply a tailored summary for each new article.

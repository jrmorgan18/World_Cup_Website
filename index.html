Why Dual Eights

“Two advanced midfielders. Two Baltimore legends. A publication built between the lines.”

The name starts with soccer tactics and ends with two number eights who define Baltimore sporting greatness. The coverage lives in that overlap: local loyalty, global curiosity and a belief that the smartest stories are rarely the loudest ones.

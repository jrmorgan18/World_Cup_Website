The cycle at a glance

The moments that move the program.

The timeline will evolve as dates become official and each checkpoint produces new evidence about the direction of the team.

Summer 2026
Baseline

The post-tournament reset

Establish the starting point: leadership, player pool and the lessons that survive the World Cup.

2027
Checkpoint

The first full competitive test

Measure the new cycle against meaningful opposition and the first sustained roster choices.

Summer 2028
Critical checkpoint

Qualifying, Copa and the Olympics

A possible collision of three competitions will test the coach, the roster and the entire federation.

2029
Pressure

The roster begins to close

Roles harden, experiments narrow and the genuine position battles come into focus.

2030
Destination

World Cup

The final assessment of what the program built and what it still could not solve.

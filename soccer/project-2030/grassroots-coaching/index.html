Where this came from

The practices are adapted from the Massachusetts Youth Soccer session plans and their guide for new volunteer coaches, which is what U.S. Soccer points beginners toward. The diagrams here are our own. Anything to do with match day - how many players, whether there's a keeper, what happens when the ball goes out - comes from your own league, so check with them.

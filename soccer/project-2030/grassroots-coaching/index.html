If the answer is yes, most of the rest takes care of itself. Everything below is the accumulated advice for volunteer coaches running a U8 team for the first time.

Where this comes from

The weekly sessions are condensed from the Massachusetts Youth Soccer U8 session-plan library and its Beginner Volunteer Coach Survival Guide, which U.S. Soccer's Soccer Forward resource hub points volunteer coaches toward. Check your own league's rules for anything to do with match day.

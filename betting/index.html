House rules

How a bet gets on the board

  • A price, not a pick. Every play is written with the number attached. The same bet is worth taking at one price and worth passing at another.
  • Break-even first. The odds convert to the win rate the bet needs. If the estimate isn't clearly above it, there's no play.
  • The case against. The strongest argument on the other side goes in the article, not the comments.
  • One unit. Everything is graded flat, so the record reflects the reads and not the sizing.
